CS ASSET SOURCES — Cake Client 6.4

The 22 selected meshes and per-knife skeletal clips come from the original
CS2 exports published at https://github.com/zh667/ScCsgoKnives .
The exact upstream commit, source file paths, clip names, inspection events
and SHA-256 hashes are in assets/cakeclient/knives/animation-provenance-v7.json.
Upstream declares GPL-3.0. Its full license and unmodified tables/meshes are
included in tools/original-cs in META-INF/cakeclient-project.zip.
No upstream implementation code is incorporated. Cake's parser, converter,
rendering and native Minecraft arm adapter are independently written.

Counter-Strike models, materials and clips retain Valve and respective
creator rights; they are not covered by Cake code's MIT license. Cake Client
is unofficial and is not endorsed by Valve. The 16 procedural Cake silhouettes
from 6.2 have been replaced, not renamed or passed off as original assets.

The four existing 2048 material sets retain their Craftprofit sources:
https://craftprofit.top/3d/skins3d.json
https://craftprofit.top/3d/w/knife_{karambit,butterfly,bayonet,bowie}_{color,rough,metal,ao}
Other entries use the unscaled CS2 color and ORM textures from upstream's
Assets/Textures/ScCsgoKnives. ORM channels are packed as Roughness/Metalness/AO
for Cake's shader. No fabricated textures or generated silhouettes are used
for the new knives. Preview images render these genuine meshes/materials.

Original source data is included. Smooth quaternion interpolation
keeps each moving part rigid. A camera framing translation keeps the support
fist visible; the left Shadow Dagger gets the same correction to retain grip.
The cubic Minecraft arms are retargeted for
grip and visibility and cannot reproduce CS2 finger articulation. Holding G
on Butterfly joins the original intro/outro sequences at their exported
inspection pause markers. This sequencing is a Cake control feature.

6.4 adds a fixed 180-degree grip correction to the right Shadow Dagger,
about its source weapon anchor. Original input geometry/clips remain bundled.

6.5 audio: 61 original Counter-Strike recordings from https://github.com/sourcesounds/csgo .
Pinned revision, Git blob hashes and converted OGG hashes in tools/original-cs/audio/provenance.json.
Original WAVs are included with source; these recordings retain their respective owners' rights.
Legacy CS recordings are mapped to identified action events; this is not a complete CS2 audio extraction.
